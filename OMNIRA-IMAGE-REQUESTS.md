## 1. Executive Protocol & Design System

The Omnira Valet visual direction is strictly anchored in a **Luxury Noir** automotive and hospitality aesthetic:
- **Palette**: Deep noir (`#0A0A0C`, `#0E0E11`), brushed graphite (`#141418`, `#1A1A20`), subtle luxury champagne/gold accents (`#C9A24A`, `#E0C273`), crisp warm-white typography (`#F5ECDB`).
- **Tone**: Understated Saudi high-end hospitality, discreet executive mobility, prestigious architectural context (King Abdullah Financial District - KAFD, luxury Riyadh/Jeddah hotel entrances).
- **Lighting**: Cinematic night/dusk low-key lighting, rim highlights reflecting off polished vehicle bodywork, subtle warm architectural glow, zero garish neon, zero flat daylight stock.
- **Rule on AI Generation**: **NO AI IMAGES ARE GENERATED YET.** All prompts below are formatted for Manager review and explicit sign-off before any image generation tooling is triggered.
